Blumea

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Blumea ("Blumea", "we", "us") provides analytics and content-understanding tools for social media creators. This policy explains what we collect from the accounts you connect, why, and how you can control or remove it.

1. What we collect

  • Account information you give us directly (name, email) when you sign up.
  • Metrics and metadata from the social accounts you explicitly connect (e.g. views, likes, comments, watch time, video titles and IDs), pulled through each platform's official API and only after you grant permission.
  • Content-understanding results: structured information our analysis produces about a video (topic, format, what happens and when), derived from the video itself.

2. How we process your videos

To analyze a video, Blumea may temporarily access the video file (directly from the platform, or from a file you choose to upload) and pass it to an AI video-understanding service to extract the structured information above. Full video files are used only for the duration of that processing and are never stored permanently. We keep the resulting structured analysis, metrics, and metadata — not the video file itself.

3. Connected accounts and access tokens

When you connect a platform account (e.g. YouTube), we request only the specific, limited permissions needed to read your own metrics and video list — never permissions to post, message, or modify anything on your behalf. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never visible in plain form, including to our own staff through normal account administration. Disconnecting an account revokes our access with that platform immediately and removes the stored access token.

4. Sharing with other creators (opt-in only)

Blumea does not include your content or metrics in any comparison, benchmark, or "trending" feature shown to other users unless you explicitly opt in. Any such feature only ever uses your own connected content — never another creator's videos or files.

5. Who else sees your data

We share the minimum necessary data with the service providers that make Blumea work: our database/hosting provider, and the AI provider used for video understanding. We do not sell your data, and we do not use unofficial scrapers or downloaders to access platform content.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing admin@blumea.io; we will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable time, except where we're required to keep something for legal or security reasons.

7. Takedown and misuse reports

If you believe your content, likeness, or account is being used on Blumea without authorization, contact admin@blumea.io with details. We will investigate and remove content or access found to violate this policy or the rights of others.

8. Children's privacy

Blumea is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn we have, we will delete it.

9. International users

If you're in the EU/EEA, UK, or Israel, you have rights over your personal data under applicable law (including access, correction, and deletion). Contact us to exercise them.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change and update the date above. Material changes will be communicated to active users.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: admin@blumea.io
